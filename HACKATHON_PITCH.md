# ForensicLens — Two-Minute Pitch

Imagine an investigator receiving eight hours of CCTV footage after an incident. Today, they must manually watch the footage, copy files between tools, and later explain whether the evidence was altered. That costs time and makes the investigative trail difficult to defend.

ForensicLens turns that process into one trustworthy workflow. An investigator loads evidence and immediately records a cryptographic hash and chain of custody. Our video-intelligence layer detects people and vehicles, creates timestamped, evidence-linked review events, and prioritizes what deserves attention.

But we do not ask investigators to blindly trust AI. Every alert is labelled as an AI-assisted triage signal, exposes its confidence and source, and can be confirmed, rejected, or marked for follow-up with an investigator’s reason and timestamp. Track search returns only class-and-time candidates—it never claims to identify a person.

ForensicLens also includes an authenticity-risk screen that flags simple visual discontinuities for human review, not as proof of tampering. When evidence must be shared, it produces a separately hashed privacy-preserving derivative while the original source remains immutable.

In the demo, we take a sample VIRAT surveillance feed from raw evidence through hash verification, AI analysis, reviewable alerts, a human decision, and a standardized report in a few clicks.

Our goal is simple: help investigators reach the right footage faster, while preserving the integrity, explainability, and human accountability needed for digital evidence.

**ForensicLens: faster CCTV triage, without compromising evidentiary trust.**
